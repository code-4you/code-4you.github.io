The Arrivals Hall

A first-person walkable 3D hub — a small sci-fi mall that is the arrivals hall of a human–AI community. Each door launches one of the games. Talk to Elara the guide, tell the fountain what you want to do, take a job, hear a story.

Choose Neighbors

Homes and communities designed around friendship — people choose their neighbours. That's all; everything else is supplementary. The full source of chooseneighbors.com: a working site, the Belonging essay series, guides and live experiments.

Visit the siteSource

World Migration Flows

An open-source, interactive map of global migration, 1960–2024 — a rebuild of Max Galka's Metrocosm global immigration map, whose original site is no longer online. Animated particles show estimated flows between countries.

Open the mapSource

World Travel Flows

An animated map of every cross-border trip on Earth, 1995–2022 — tourism, business, family visits, short-term work. The sibling of World Migration Flows: together the two maps tile all movement between countries.

Open the mapSource

Script to Screen

Turn any script into a voiced, illustrated video — free, in your browser, no signup. Paste a screenplay or story: it works out who speaks each line, gives every character its own voice, reads it aloud, sketches the visuals and hands you a single video file.

Open the toolSource

Visits vs Migration

Which travel corridors are pathways, and which are just tourism? Every corridor on Earth coloured by migrations per 10,000 visits, 1995–2022 — cold blue for pure visiting, hot pink for a migration corridor.
